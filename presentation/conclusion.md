# Reflection
- Worked iteratively across research, design, and implementation
- Continuous iteration helped maintain direction as complexity increased
- Clear prioritisation supported team alignment

# Lessons Learned 

## Research & Design
- Letting interview findings drive decisions reduced assumptions
- Mapping features directly to identified themes strengthened our design decisions

## Iteration & Usability
- Usability testing showed that functional clarity does not guarantee emotional engagement
- Familiar patterns improved learnability but need visual identity to create attachment

## Data & Refactoring
- Using TypeScript helped us think about data structures and database models early in the process
- Refactoring was necessary as complexity grew and helped clarify earlier decisions
- Earlier database planning would have reduced later rework

## Collaboration
- Ongoing discussion of design decisions made adapting to change easier
- Shared understanding of goals supported smooth iteration across disciplines
